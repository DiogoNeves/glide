# Daily Founder Check-In

Status: Proposed

Suggested cadence: daily or weekdays.

Skill: `glide-daily-founder-check-in`

Purpose: surface one useful company signal, question, risk, opportunity, or follow-through nudge. Rank competing signals by deadline, stakes, source reliability, and whether the founder can act today. Use two or three items only when each is genuinely urgent or very important.

Do not enable without confirmation.
