# Company Context Setup

Use when installing Glide or creating the first useful company context.

## Process

1. Inspect available company materials before asking questions.
2. Draft `Company Context.md` and `Founder Brief.md`.
3. Ask what is wrong, missing, uncertain, and strategically important right now.
4. Capture exact customer language when available.
5. Mark uncertain claims as uncertain.
6. Keep the first version useful, not exhaustive.

## Key Sections

- Company overview.
- Product.
- Customers and ICP.
- Market and competition.
- GTM.
- Metrics.
- Strategy.
- Team and operating model.
- Fundraising and board context.
- Open questions.
