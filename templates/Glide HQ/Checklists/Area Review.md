# Area Review

Purpose: review one company area.

Follow [Input and Collaboration](Input%20and%20Collaboration.md) for owner-facing questions, reviews and optional shared documents. Keep internal file maintenance with the agent.

## Process

1. Load `Company Context.md`, `Founder Brief.md`, and the area files.
2. Identify the area's current objective, context, decisions, questions, and follow-through.
3. Check cross-company dependencies.
4. Find risks, contradictions, missing evidence, and next moves.
5. Recommend small updates to the area files.

## Output

- What matters.
- Risks or contradictions.
- Missing context.
- Suggested next move.
- Files to update.
