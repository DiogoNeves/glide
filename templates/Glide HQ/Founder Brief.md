# Founder Brief

High-level context about the founder or CEO for Glide to load when giving advice, creating decisions, planning, or running check-ins.

This is not a diary. Keep it concise and operational.

## Stable Context

- Role, responsibilities, values, leadership style, constraints, and durable preferences.

## Current Context

- Current priorities, stressors, capacity, deadlines, sensitive boundaries, or tradeoffs.

## Risk Appetite

- Default level of risk, speed, spending, hiring, fundraising, public communication, and delegation.

## Communication Preferences

- How the founder wants advice, questions, disagreement, and summaries.

## Approval Boundaries

- Reading and fetching information is allowed when configured.
- Drafting is allowed.
- Ask before external changes, posts, sends, commitments, purchases, approvals, deletions, scheduling, or publishing.
